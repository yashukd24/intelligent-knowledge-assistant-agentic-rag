=== APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY ===
UNDERGRADUATE ADMISSIONS & ACADEMIC PROGRAMS INFORMATION (2025-2026)

SECTION 1: AVAILABLE UNDERGRADUATE COURSES:
Apex Institute offers full-time 4-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree programs in:
1. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) — Approved Intake: 180 seats.
2. Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI&DS) — Approved Intake: 120 seats.
3. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) — Approved Intake: 120 seats.
4. Mechanical Engineering (ME) — Approved Intake: 60 seats.
5. Civil Engineering (CE) — Approved Intake: 60 seats.

SECTION 2: ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
1. Academic Qualifications: The candidate must have passed the 10+2 examination or its equivalent recognized by the state/central board with a minimum of 60.0% aggregate marks in Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry (or Computer Science / Biology).
2. Qualifying Examination: Valid rank in National JEE Main or State Common Entrance Test (CET).

SECTION 3: MANDATORY DOCUMENTS NEEDED FOR ADMISSION:
Candidates must present the following original documents along with three sets of self-attested copies at the time of counseling and enrollment:
1. Class 10th (Secondary School) Passing Certificate and Mark Sheet (for proof of date of birth).
2. Class 12th (Higher Secondary) Mark Sheet and Passing Certificate.
3. Entrance Examination Scorecard and Admit Card.
4. Transfer Certificate (TC) and Conduct Certificate from the last attended institution.
5. Migration Certificate (for students from boards other than the state board).
6. Caste / Community Certificate (for SC, ST, OBC, or EWS category reservation applicants).
7. Copy of Aadhar Card and 5 recent passport-sized color photographs.

SECTION 4: CAMPUS FACILITIES:
- On-campus high-speed Wi-Fi network (1 Gbps leased line).
- Separate modern hostels for boys (500 beds) and girls (400 beds) with round-the-clock security and dining mess.
- Fully equipped gymnasiums, basketball court, indoor badminton stadium, and Olympic-size running track.
- 24/7 Health Care Center with a resident medical officer and ambulance on standby.
