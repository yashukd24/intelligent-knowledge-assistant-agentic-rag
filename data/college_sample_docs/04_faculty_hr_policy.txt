=== APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY ===
FACULTY LEAVE REGULATIONS & HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY

SECTION 1: CASUAL LEAVE (CL) PROCEDURE:
1. Full-time faculty members are entitled to twelve (12) days of Casual Leave (CL) per calendar year.
2. A faculty member may avail a maximum of three (3) consecutive days of Casual Leave at one time. Any absence exceeding three days requires sanction from the Principal.
3. Leave applications must be submitted through the Institutional ERP portal at least three (3) working days prior to the proposed leave date.
4. Alternate class arrangements: The applicant must formally designate substitute faculty colleagues to cover all scheduled lecture classes, laboratory sessions, and tutorial hours during the leave period.

SECTION 2: ACADEMIC LEAVE (AL):
1. Up to ten (10) days of Academic Leave per academic year is sanctioned for faculty members participating in peer-reviewed national or international conferences (such as IEEE, ACM, or Springer), presenting accepted research papers, or serving on university examination boards.
2. Proof of paper acceptance and conference registration must accompany the academic leave application.

SECTION 3: EMERGENCY MEDICAL LEAVE:
In cases of unexpected hospitalization or severe illness, the faculty member or their nominee must notify the HOD via email within 24 hours. A valid medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner must be produced upon resuming duties.
