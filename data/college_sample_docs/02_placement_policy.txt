=== APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY ===
CAMPUS PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY & REGULATIONS (ACADEMIC YEAR 2025-2026)

POLICY SCOPE & GOVERNANCE:
This document establishes authoritative institutional guidelines governing undergraduate and postgraduate campus recruitment drives conducted by the Training and Placement Cell.

SECTION 1: PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS:
To participate in campus recruitment drives, students must strictly fulfill all of the following conditions:
1. Minimum Academic Performance: Students must maintain a cumulative aggregate of 65.0% or higher (or a CGPA of 6.75 on a 10.0 scale) across all completed semesters up to the 6th semester.
2. Backlog Regulation: Students must have zero (0) active backlogs at the time of registering for any campus recruitment drive. All historical backlogs must be cleared prior to 7th semester registration.
3. Training Attendance: Mandatory attendance of at least 80% is required across all institutional pre-placement training modules, aptitude bootcamps, and mock technical interview rounds.
4. Professional Conduct: Registered candidates who fail to attend scheduled interview rounds without 24-hour advance written intimation shall be debarred from campus placements for the remainder of the placement season.

SECTION 2: OFFER CATEGORIES & ONE-STUDENT-ONE-OFFER RULE:
- Category A (Dream Offer): Recruitment offers with Cost-to-Company (CTC) equal to or exceeding 8.0 Lakhs Per Annum (8.0 LPA).
- Category B (Regular Offer): Offers with CTC below 8.0 LPA.
- Once a student secures a Dream Offer (CTC >= 8.0 LPA), they become ineligible to participate in subsequent campus drives to ensure equitable opportunity across the graduating cohort.
