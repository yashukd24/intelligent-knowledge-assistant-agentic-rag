=== APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY ===
REVISED DRAFT CAMPUS PLACEMENT REGULATIONS (VERSION 2.0 - DRAFT FOR REVIEW)

NOTICE ON POLICY VERSION EVOLUTION:
This document represents Version 2.0 of the Campus Placement Regulations, proposed by the Placement Board for Academic Year 2026-2027.

SECTION 1: REVISED ELIGIBILITY THRESHOLD (CONFLICTING WITH V1.0):
Under this revised draft version:
1. Minimum Academic Requirement: The minimum cumulative aggregate required to register for campus placements is raised to 70.0% (or a CGPA of 7.25 on a 10.0 scale), amending the earlier 65.0% requirement specified in Version 1.0 (AY 2025-2026).
2. Dream Offer Threshold: The CTC definition for Dream Offers is updated from 8.0 LPA to 10.0 LPA.

ADMINISTRATIVE STATUS:
Version 1.0 remains the legally active policy for the graduating batch of 2025-2026. Version 2.0 is subject to final ratification by the Academic Council for 2026-2027.
