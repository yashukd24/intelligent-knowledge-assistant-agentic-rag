=== APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY ===
UNDERGRADUATE INTERNSHIP GUIDELINES & ACADEMIC CREDIT RULES

SECTION 1: MANDATORY SUMMER INTERNSHIP REQUIREMENT:
All enrolled undergraduate engineering students must complete a mandatory 8-week summer industrial internship in an accredited corporate enterprise, research organization, or institutional laboratory following the completion of the 6th semester examinations and prior to entering the 7th semester.

SECTION 2: APPROVAL AND NO OBJECTION CERTIFICATE (NOC):
1. Prior to commencing any external internship, students must obtain a formal No Objection Certificate (NOC) endorsed by the respective Head of Department (HOD) and the Dean of Academic Affairs.
2. Unapproved or retroactive internships will not be recognized for institutional academic credit.

SECTION 3: REPORTING AND EVALUATION:
1. Every intern must maintain a digital weekly progress log summarizing technical activities and project deliverables, signed by their designated industrial mentor.
2. Weekly logs must be submitted via the college ERP portal by 5:00 PM every Monday.
3. Upon returning to campus for the 7th semester, students must present a comprehensive technical report and undergo an oral defense before the Departmental Internship Evaluation Committee. The internship carries three (3) academic credits.
